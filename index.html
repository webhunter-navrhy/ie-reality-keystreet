Description

Welcome to 9 Churchwell Grove, a superbly located four-bedroom, two-bathroom residence offering spacious and versatile living accommodation in the heart of Belmayne, one of Dublin 13’s most desirable residential neighbourhoods.

Step inside to discover a large, welcoming living room, perfect for family gatherings, relaxed entertaining, and modern day-to-day living. The property also benefits from an excellent kitchen area, generously sized and designed with both functionality and social living in mind — ideal for meal preparation, casual dining, and hosting friends and family.

All four bedrooms are well-proportioned and adaptable for use as bedrooms, guest rooms, or home offices, while the two bathrooms provide added convenience for busy households.

Prime Location & Connectivity

Situated just off the Malahide Road, this home enjoys superb access to a wide variety of local amenities including shops, cafés, schools, creches, and leisure facilities all within easy reach. Clarehall Shopping Centre and Donaghmede Shopping Centre are both nearby, providing a great choice of retail and services right on your doorstep.

For commuters and those on the move, excellent transport links make this location particularly appealing:

Clongriffin DART station, with services into Dublin City Centre and beyond, is within walking distance, enabling easy access to the wider rail network.

A frequent network of Dublin Bus routes (including services along the Malahide Road corridor) connects you swiftly to key destinations across the city.

The nearby M50 and M1 motorways ensure smooth road travel to Dublin Airport (approximately 15 minutes) and other major commuter routes, while quality bus corridors further enhance accessibility.
